How it works
Everyone gets six cards face down in two rows. Each turn you do one thing: flip one of your face-down cards, or take the top discard and swap it for one of your cards, or draw from the deck and either swap it in or drop it on the discard pile. Two of the same in a column cancel out. When someone has all six face up, everyone else gets one last turn. Lowest score wins. Two to four players.

Ace 1 · 2–10 face value · Jack, Queen, King 10 · Joker 0
